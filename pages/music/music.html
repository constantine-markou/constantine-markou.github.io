About My Music

I began creating music in late 2025, following an expansive exploration of both emerging and established movements and genres.

Music then became a prominent creative focus of mine going forward. I spent 6 months teaching myself music theory, often through dissecting songs I found interesting.

I then learnt how to utilise a DAW (Mine being FL Studio) to create music, this part was by far the most tricky, but I soon began creating music of my own.
